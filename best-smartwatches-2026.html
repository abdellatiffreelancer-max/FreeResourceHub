SMARTWATCH BUYING GUIDE

Best Smartwatches in 2026

A practical guide to choosing a smartwatch with the right features for everyday use, fitness and productivity.

Smartwatches have become useful everyday devices for checking notifications, tracking activity, monitoring workouts and staying connected. However, choosing the right model can be difficult because different watches focus on different features.


In this guide, we look at several popular smartwatch options and explain what makes each one worth considering.

1. Apple Watch Series

Apple Watch models are a strong option for people who use an iPhone and want a smartwatch that works smoothly with the Apple ecosystem.

  • Excellent integration with iPhone
  • Useful health and fitness features
  • Large selection of apps
  • Clear and responsive display
  • Strong notification and communication features

2. Samsung Galaxy Watch Series

Samsung Galaxy Watch models are designed for Android users who want a combination of smart features, fitness tracking and a modern design.

  • Good Android integration
  • Modern and attractive design
  • Fitness and activity tracking
  • Useful health-related features
  • Bright and responsive displays
Check Price

3. Google Pixel Watch Series

Google Pixel Watch models are a good choice for Android users who prefer Google's software ecosystem and a simple smartwatch experience.

  • Clean and simple interface
  • Good integration with Google services
  • Fitness and activity tracking
  • Compact and modern design
  • Useful smart notifications
Check Price

4. Garmin Smartwatches

Garmin watches are particularly interesting for people who care about fitness, running, outdoor activities and detailed workout information.

  • Excellent fitness tracking
  • Strong GPS capabilities
  • Useful sports features
  • Long battery life on many models
  • Detailed workout information
Check Price

5. Amazfit Smartwatches

Amazfit offers a variety of smartwatches that focus on useful features and long battery life, often at more accessible price points.

  • Good value for money
  • Long battery life on many models
  • Fitness and health tracking
  • Large selection of models
  • Useful everyday smartwatch features
Check Price

How to Choose the Right Smartwatch

The best smartwatch depends on your smartphone, budget and the features you actually need. iPhone users may prefer Apple Watch, while Android users have many options from Samsung, Google, Garmin and other brands.


Before buying, consider compatibility, battery life, fitness features, display quality, comfort and the functions you will use most often.
