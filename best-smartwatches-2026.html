1. Apple Watch Series 11

Best for: iPhone users

The Apple Watch Series 11 is a strong choice for people who already use an iPhone and want a smartwatch that integrates closely with Apple's ecosystem.

  • Strong integration with iPhone
  • Wide range of fitness and activity features
  • Useful notifications and everyday smart features
  • Large Apple Watch app ecosystem
Things to consider: Apple Watch is primarily designed for the Apple ecosystem, so Android users should look at other options.

2. Samsung Galaxy Watch8

Best for: Android and Samsung users

The Galaxy Watch8 is a versatile Android smartwatch combining fitness tracking, notifications, apps and Samsung ecosystem integration.

  • Strong Android integration
  • Fitness and activity tracking
  • Useful health and wellness features
  • Modern smartwatch design
Things to consider: Some features and functionality can depend on the connected phone and region, so check compatibility before purchasing.

3. Google Pixel Watch 4

Best for: Google ecosystem users

The Pixel Watch 4 is designed around Google's smartwatch and Fitbit ecosystem, making it an attractive option for Android users who want a clean Google experience.

  • Google-focused software experience
  • Fitbit fitness and activity features
  • Useful Google services and notifications

Check Pixel Watch 4

How to Choose the Right Smartwatch

  • Phone compatibility: Start by checking whether the watch works properly with your smartphone.
  • Fitness tracking: If workouts are important, look for reliable activity, GPS and training features.
  • Battery life: Consider how often you are willing to charge the watch.
  • Smart features: Notifications, calls, apps, payments and voice assistants can make a watch much more useful for everyday tasks.
  • Health features: Compare the health and wellness functions that are actually available in your country and compatible with your phone.
  • Comfort: A smartwatch is something you may wear for many hours, so weight, size and strap comfort matter.
  • Ecosystem: Choosing a watch that works well with the services you already use can make the overall experience much better.
