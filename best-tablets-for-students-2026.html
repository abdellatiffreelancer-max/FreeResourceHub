Best Tablets for Students in 2026

Find a tablet that can handle lectures, note-taking, research, reading, video calls and everyday entertainment without replacing your laptop unnecessarily.

What Should Students Look For in a Tablet?

A good student tablet should be easy to carry, have a comfortable screen for reading and provide enough performance for everyday applications. Stylus support can also be important for handwritten notes, PDFs and diagrams.

The best choice depends on your course, operating system preference, budget and whether the tablet will be used alongside a laptop or as a more independent computer.

01 — Best Overall

Apple iPad (A16)

The standard iPad with the A16 chip is a versatile option for students who want a reliable tablet for reading, browsing, note-taking, video calls and general study tasks.

  • Apple A16 chip
  • 11-inch Liquid Retina display
  • Supports Apple Pencil
  • USB-C connectivity
  • iPadOS multitasking features
  • Available with Wi-Fi and cellular options

Best for: students who want a balanced tablet for studying, note-taking and everyday use.
