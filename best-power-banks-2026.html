POWER BANK BUYING GUIDE

Best Power Banks in 2026

A practical guide to choosing a power bank based on capacity, charging speed, portability, laptop support, wireless charging and overall value.

A power bank can be one of the most useful accessories for smartphones, tablets, laptops and other portable devices.

Modern power banks are no longer limited to basic phone charging. Some models can deliver enough power for laptops, while others focus on compact designs, wireless charging or built-in cables.

In this guide, we compare several power banks designed for different situations and explain what to look for before buying one.

What to Consider Before Buying

The right power bank depends on the devices you need to charge and how you plan to use it.

  • Capacity: measured in mAh, capacity indicates how much stored energy the power bank can provide.
  • Output power: higher wattage can provide faster charging and may be necessary for compatible laptops.
  • Portability: larger-capacity models are usually heavier, so consider how often you will carry the power bank.
  • Ports: USB-C and USB-A ports allow you to connect different devices and sometimes charge several devices at once.
  • Built-in cables: integrated cables can reduce the need to carry an additional charging cable.
  • Wireless charging: magnetic and Qi2-compatible models can provide convenient wireless charging for supported devices.
  • Travel requirements: if you fly frequently, check the power bank's Wh rating and the airline's current rules before travel.

How to Choose the Right Power Bank

If you mainly need to recharge a smartphone during the day, a compact 10,000mAh model can be easier to carry than a large high-capacity power bank.

If you regularly charge laptops, tablets and multiple devices, a higher-output model such as the Anker Prime or UGREEN Nexode can make more sense.

Wireless and magnetic models can be especially convenient for compatible phones, while built-in cables can reduce the number of accessories you need to carry.

Before buying, check the capacity, output wattage, supported charging standards, ports, weight and compatibility with your devices.
