How to Choose the Right Power Bank

Capacity and charging speed are important, but they are not the only factors worth considering before buying.

  • Capacity: A 10,000mAh model is convenient for everyday backup, while larger capacities are better suited to extended travel and multiple devices.
  • Output power: Higher wattage can allow compatible devices to charge faster. Check what your phone, tablet or laptop actually supports.
  • Ports: If you regularly charge several devices, consider how many ports you need.
  • Built-in cables: Integrated cables can reduce the number of accessories you need to carry.
  • Wireless charging: Qi2-compatible models can be convenient for compatible phones and accessories.
  • Size and weight: A larger capacity normally means a larger and heavier device. Choose according to how you plan to carry it.
  • Device compatibility: Always check the power requirements of your devices before purchasing.
