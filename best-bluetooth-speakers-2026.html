How to Choose a Bluetooth Speaker

The right Bluetooth speaker depends on where you plan to use it and what you expect from the listening experience.

  • Sound quality: Consider the type of music you listen to and whether you prefer powerful bass, balanced sound or a more detailed presentation.
  • Size and portability: Smaller speakers are easier to carry, while larger models can generally produce a bigger and more powerful sound.
  • Battery life: If you frequently use your speaker away from home, battery performance becomes an important factor.
  • Durability: For outdoor use, look for a speaker designed to handle dust, splashes or other environmental conditions.
  • Connectivity: Check Bluetooth compatibility and any additional connectivity features that you may need.
  • Use case: A speaker for a bedroom does not need the same characteristics as one intended for parties, travel or outdoor activities.
  • Charging: Consider how convenient the speaker is to recharge and whether its charging system fits your existing setup.
