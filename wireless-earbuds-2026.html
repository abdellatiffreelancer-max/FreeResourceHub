What to Look For Before Buying

Specifications are useful, but the best earbuds should match how you actually plan to use them.

1. Sound Quality

Consider overall tuning, clarity, bass response and whether the earbuds support high-quality audio features that are compatible with your devices.

2. Noise Cancellation

ANC can make a major difference on public transport, during travel or in busy offices. Performance varies between models and environments.

3. Comfort & Fit

Even excellent earbuds are not useful if they do not fit comfortably. Consider the shape, ear tips and stability during movement.

4. Battery Life

Check both the listening time of the earbuds and the additional battery provided by the charging case.

5. Smartphone Ecosystem

Some features work best within a particular ecosystem. iPhone users may benefit from AirPods, while Samsung users may prefer Galaxy Buds.

6. Water & Sweat Resistance

If you plan to exercise or use earbuds outdoors, check the manufacturer's official resistance rating before purchasing.
